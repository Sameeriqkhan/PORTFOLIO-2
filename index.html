Beam Sizing Automation (Shear/Moment & Diameter Design)

Problem: Quickly generate shear/moment diagrams and size a circular cross‑section so principal stress ≤ allowable along a two‑span beam.

Approach: Wrote vmd.m to compute/plot V and M for unequal spans; stress.m to return τmax, σb,max, and σ1; built caseEqual.m for l₁=l₂ minimal diameter and caseGeneral.m sweeping 50 l₁ values (2–10 cm) and taking the envelope.

Result: 100‑point diameter profile that satisfies allowable stress at every location; auto‑generated plots for V/M and diameter design for reports.
