Overview

This project focused on designing a practical bottle holder that could be printed, tested, and adjusted quickly. The goal was a clean everyday-use design with secure fit, easy access, and a lightweight structure.

What I Built

  • CAD model of the holder body and storage features.
  • FDM-printed prototypes for fit and strength checks.
  • Geometry for bottle retention and quick removal.
  • Lightweight structure with practical everyday-carry features.

Design Notes

  • Adjusted wall thickness to balance stiffness and weight.
  • Used fillets and chamfers for comfort and stress reduction.
  • Checked fit against common bottle dimensions.
  • Considered print orientation and layer strength around high-load areas.

Key Takeaway

The project showed how small changes in wall thickness, clearances, and print orientation can make a simple product feel more reliable and usable. Iterative print testing helped refine the final form.
