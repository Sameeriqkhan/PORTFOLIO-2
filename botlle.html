Water Bottle Holder

Ergonomic everyday-carry holder designed in SolidWorks and optimized for 3D printing. Focus on secure grip, quick access, and compatibility with common bottle sizes.

SolidWorks Rapid Prototyping DFM / Printability

Problem

Carry bottles securely with quick access, without bulky cages or straps.

Approach

Iterative CAD → print → test; tuned wall thickness, ribs, fillets; ergonomic grip and latch.

Result

Lightweight, durable holder with stable fit and fast retrieval during daily use.

Build Notes

  • Printed prototypes (FDM) to validate stiffness vs. weight; adjusted infill and perimeters for strength.
  • Chamfers/fillets for comfort and reduced stress concentrations.
  • Snap-fit + optional strap points for bags/belts; accepts common bottle diameters.
  • Modeled tolerances for shrink/warp; oriented for strong layer adhesion at the latch interface.
